Broken Rainbow PAYS 15 TO 1 How to Hit: Triggered when both Player and Banker stand immediately on their initial 2 cards (4 total cards dealt in the round, zero draw cards). This occurs when Player's first 2 cards match color AND Banker's first 2 cards match color. Rules & Overrides: Pays 15:1 whether the two matched pairs are the same color or different colors, and regardless of Player win, Banker win, or Tie. 3. Natural Tie PAYS 35 TO 1 How to Hit: Triggered when a Broken Rainbow occurs (both sides stand on 2 matched-color cards, 4 total cards dealt) AND Player and Banker finish with the exact same point total (0–0, 1–1, or 2–2). Rules & Overrides: Stacks with Standard Tie (2:1) and Broken Rainbow (15:1), while main Player/Banker wagers push. 4. Player Rainbow PAYS 10 TO 1 How to Hit: Triggered when the Player hand draws to 4 cards and finishes with all 4 distinct colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green). Banker Rainbow PAYS 10 TO 1 How to Hit: Triggered when the Banker hand draws to 4 cards and finishes with all 4 distinct colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green). Rules & Overrides: Wins 10:1 whether Banker wins, loses, or ties—except if Player also hits a 4-color rainbow (Double Rainbow / Pot O' Gold), in which case Banker Rainbow pushes (stake returned). 6. Double Rainbow PAYS 100 TO 1 How to Hit: Triggered when BOTH Player and Banker draw to 4 cards and BOTH hands finish with all 4 distinct colors (8 total cards, two complete 4-color rainbows), provided the score is NOT a 4–4 Ace tie.
