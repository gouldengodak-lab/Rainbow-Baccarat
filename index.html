🌈 Every Rainbow Baccarat bonus wager is resolved automatically using the spectral colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green) and card counts of the round.
1. Mini Rainbow PAYS 8 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered on the initial 4 cards dealt (P1, B1, P2, B2) when all 4 cards are 4 distinct colors (1 Red, 1 Blue, 1 Yellow, 1 Green).

Rules & Overrides: Judged strictly on the initial 4-card flop before any draw cards are dealt. Never pushes and pays 8:1 regardless of who wins the hand.

2. Broken Rainbow PAYS 15 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered when both Player and Banker stand immediately on their initial 2 cards (4 total cards dealt in the round, zero draw cards). This occurs when Player's first 2 cards match color AND Banker's first 2 cards match color.

Rules & Overrides: Pays 15:1 whether the two matched pairs are the same color or different colors, and regardless of Player win, Banker win, or Tie.

3. Natural Tie PAYS 35 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered when a Broken Rainbow occurs (both sides stand on 2 matched-color cards, 4 total cards dealt) AND Player and Banker finish with the exact same point total (0–0, 1–1, or 2–2).

Rules & Overrides: Stacks with Standard Tie (2:1) and Broken Rainbow (15:1), while main Player/Banker wagers push.

4. Player Rainbow PAYS 10 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered when the Player hand draws to 4 cards and finishes with all 4 distinct colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green).

Rules & Overrides: Wins 10:1 whether Player wins, loses, or ties—except if Banker also hits a 4-color rainbow (Double Rainbow / Pot O' Gold), in which case Player Rainbow pushes (stake returned).

5. Banker Rainbow PAYS 10 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered when the Banker hand draws to 4 cards and finishes with all 4 distinct colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green).

Rules & Overrides: Wins 10:1 whether Banker wins, loses, or ties—except if Player also hits a 4-color rainbow (Double Rainbow / Pot O' Gold), in which case Banker Rainbow pushes (stake returned).

6. Double Rainbow PAYS 100 TO 1

How to Hit: Triggered when BOTH Player and Banker draw to 4 cards and BOTH hands finish with all 4 distinct colors (8 total cards, two complete 4-color rainbows), provided the score is NOT a 4–4 Ace tie.

Rules & Overrides: Overrides main Player and Banker bets (both lose), pushes Player Rainbow and Banker Rainbow, and still pays Standard Tie (2:1) if the two hands tie at 0–0, 1–1, 2–2, or 3–3.

7. Pot O' Gold Progressive ($1) 100% METER

How to Hit: Triggered when BOTH Player and Banker complete a 4-color rainbow (Double Rainbow) AND every single one of those 8 cards is an Ace, creating a 4–4 Rainbow Tie.

Rules & Overrides: Awards 100% of the live LED progressive meter (seeded at $5,000.00, growing +$0.60 per $1 progressive wager). Standard Tie (2:1) also wins, Player/Banker Rainbow push, and main Player/Banker/Double Rainbow wagers are superseded by the jackpot.

1. Custom 48-Card Deck (8-Deck Shoe = 384 Cards)

Four spectral colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green). Only Aces (1 pt) and Face Cards K/Q/J (0 pts) are used (6 Aces + 6 Faces per color per deck). Hand score = count of Aces (0–4).

2. Color-Uniqueness Drawing Rules
  • Deal P1, B1, P2, B2 round-robin. Player acts first, then Banker.
  • Initial 2 cards SAME color: STAND immediately at 2 cards.
  • Initial 2 cards DIFFERENT colors: DRAW a 3rd card.
  • After 3rd card: If 3rd card matches either of first 2 colors, STAND. If all 3 colors are DIFFERENT, DRAW a 4th and final card!
